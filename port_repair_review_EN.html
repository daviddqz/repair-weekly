1. What this report is

Every month HQ pulls every repair job order raised in the period from NAW (Manage Repair, MNR_M2330) across all ports and measures how long each one has been sitting. A job order open for weeks is a container we cannot release, cannot bill and cannot reuse — so the purpose of this review is to surface stuck units early, not to rank ports.

It is a diagnostic, not a scorecard. Two ports can show the same warning count for opposite reasons: one has a slow repairer, the other is waiting for an owner to approve an estimate. Only the port knows which. That is why section 5 exists.

6. Two things that will be checked

Do not close a job order to clear the aging. Every Repair Complete is reconciled against container movement; a premature closure is re-opened and reported back to the port.
Do not leave Rejected units silent. A Rejected unit with no re-submission date is treated as an unowned container and escalated.
