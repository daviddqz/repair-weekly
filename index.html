How to read these numbers. Under Repair aging runs from Job Order Send Date, Rejected from Estimate Request Date, and Repair Complete from Estimate Request Date to completion — the three statuses are not on one clock. “All status” aging (total 10,103) and “unclosed overdue” aging (518) are a whole and a subset, and are shown separately. The two periods overlap by 29 days, so read the direction rather than the raw sums. Source is a live snapshot from ops.culines.com; totals move if any office is re-exported mid-week.
